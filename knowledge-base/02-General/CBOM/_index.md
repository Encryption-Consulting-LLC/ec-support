# CBOM

This section explains the Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM), a standard inventory of the algorithms, keys, certificates and protocols an organization uses. It covers how a CBOM is built, how it differs from a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM), and the use cases and regulations that drive it. For product steps, see the CBOM Secure section.
