# Welcome to the EC Support Portal

The Encryption Consulting (EC) support portal is the single place to get help with EC products, managed services, and advisory engagements. This article explains what the portal offers, how to get access, and where to go first. It is written for administrators, engineers, and named support contacts at customer organizations.

## What the portal offers

The portal brings together self-service content and direct access to EC support engineers. It is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for self-service, even when live support coverage depends on the support plan.

| Area | What it provides |
|---|---|
| Knowledge base | How-to guides, runbooks, troubleshooting articles, FAQs, and concept articles |
| Open a support request | A form to create a new case with severity, product, and log attachments |
| View my requests | A list of open and closed cases for the user and, if permitted, the organization |
| Product documentation | Release notes, installation guides, and administration guides for EC products |
| Featured articles | Hand-picked PKI and Hardware Security Module (HSM) runbooks |
| Popular right now | Timely articles on industry changes, such as shorter certificate lifetimes and post-quantum cryptography (PQC) |
| Training | Links to EC PKI and HSM training courses |
| Free tools | CSR Generator, ASN.1 decoder, OpenSSL decoder, and other EC tools |

## Who can use the portal

- **Named support contacts:** People listed on the support agreement. They can open cases at every severity level and receive case updates.
- **Additional portal users:** Colleagues who can read the knowledge base and, if the organization allows it, view the organization's cases.
- **Account administrators:** The customer contact who manages the user list and asks EC to add or remove named contacts.

The number of named support contacts depends on the support plan. See [Support plans and coverage](support-plans-and-coverage.md).

## Getting access

1. Look for the welcome email from EC sent at the start of the contract. It contains the portal link: [support.encryptionconsulting.com](https://support.encryptionconsulting.com).
2. Select the activation link and set a password. Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) when prompted {{TBD: confirm MFA options supported by the portal}}.
3. Sign in and confirm the organization name and support plan shown on the profile page.
4. If the welcome email never arrived, email [support@encryptionconsulting.com](mailto:support@encryptionconsulting.com) from a company email address and include the organization name and contract or order number.

> **Tip:** Ask the account administrator to keep the list of named contacts current. Cases from people who are not on the list can be delayed while EC confirms authorization.

## How a case moves through support

1. A named contact opens a case on the portal or, for urgent issues, calls the support line.
2. EC triages the case, confirms the severity, and assigns an engineer.
3. The engineer works the case and may ask for logs or a remote session.
4. EC proposes a fix, workaround, or next step.
5. The customer confirms the result, and EC closes the case. A closed case can be reopened within {{TBD: number of days a closed case can be reopened}}.
