# EC Training and Certification

Encryption Consulting (EC) offers training for teams that run PKI, Hardware Security Modules (HSMs), and EC products. This article describes the courses, who they are for, and how to enroll. Well-trained teams resolve many issues on their own and open clearer support cases.

## What the service covers

| Course area | Topics |
|---|---|
| PKI training | PKI fundamentals, certificate hierarchy design, Microsoft Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS) installation and operation, certificate templates, Certificate Revocation Lists (CRLs) and Online Certificate Status Protocol (OCSP), CA backup and recovery, AD CS security |
| HSM training: Thales Luna | Luna architecture, partitions, roles, PIN Entry Device (PED) authentication, client setup, high availability (HA) groups, backup and restore |
| HSM training: Entrust nShield | Security World concepts, Administrator Card Set (ACS) and Operator Card Set (OCS), quorum (K of N), client and Remote File System (RFS) setup, key recovery |
| EC product onboarding | Setup and daily administration of CertSecure Manager, CodeSign Secure, CBOM Secure, and SSH Secure |
| Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) awareness | NIST PQC standards (FIPS 203, 204, 205), crypto-agility, building a migration roadmap |

Course catalog and schedule: https://www.encryptionconsulting.com/training/.

## Who it is for

- PKI administrators and engineers.
- HSM custodians and security officers.
- DevOps and platform teams that use certificate automation or code signing.
- Security architects planning PQC migration.
- New team members taking over an existing PKI or HSM estate.

## Engagement phases

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Needs review | EC and the customer agree on goals, audience, and skill level |
| 2. Course selection | Choose standard courses or a tailored agenda |
| 3. Scheduling | Pick dates, delivery format (online or on-site), and time zone |
| 4. Delivery | Instructor-led sessions with hands-on labs |
| 5. Assessment | Optional knowledge check at the end of the course |
| 6. Follow-up | Q and A session and course materials for reference |

## Deliverables

- Instructor-led sessions, live online or on-site.
- Lab exercises in an EC-provided lab environment.
- Course materials and reference guides.
- Certificate of completion for each attendee.

## What EC needs from the customer

- Number of attendees and their roles.
- Current skill level and the platforms in use (for example Luna 7, nShield 5c, Windows Server 2022).
- Preferred dates and time zone.
- For on-site training: a room, network access, and any visitor approvals.

## How to request

1. Review the catalog at https://www.encryptionconsulting.com/training/.
2. Contact info@encryptionconsulting.com, or open a Severity 4 case with the subject "Training request".
3. Include the course, number of attendees, and preferred dates.

The Premium Plus support plan includes training credits. See [Support plans and coverage](support-plans-and-coverage.md).

> **Note:** Training is not delivered through support cases. Support engineers can answer specific product questions, but full courses are scheduled as a separate engagement.
