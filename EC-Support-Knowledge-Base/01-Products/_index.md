# EC Products

This section holds setup guides, how-to articles, troubleshooting, and FAQs for every Encryption Consulting (EC) product. Pick a product below to open its landing page. Each landing page starts with an overview article for new users.

## Certificate and key management

- [CertSecure Manager](CertSecure-Manager/_index.md): Certificate lifecycle management (CLM). Discovery, inventory, expiry alerts, automated renewal and deployment, ACME, and policy enforcement across Microsoft AD CS and public CAs.
- [SSH Secure](SSH-Secure/_index.md): Secure Shell (SSH) key lifecycle management. Discovery of orphaned and over-privileged keys, rotation, and compliance reporting across Linux, Unix, Windows, cloud, and Kubernetes.

## Code signing

- [CodeSign Secure](CodeSign-Secure/_index.md): Code signing with keys kept in Hardware Security Modules (HSMs), approval workflows, role-based access control, and CI/CD pipeline integration.

## Cryptographic inventory

- [CBOM Secure](CBOM-Secure/_index.md): Cryptographic discovery that produces a Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) in CycloneDX format and flags quantum-vulnerable algorithms for post-quantum cryptography (PQC) planning.

## Managed services

- [PKI-as-a-Service](PKI-as-a-Service/_index.md): Fully managed public key infrastructure (PKI) operated by EC, with HSM-backed root and issuing CAs, AD CS integration, ACME, SCEP, and Intune support.
- [HSM-as-a-Service](HSM-as-a-Service/_index.md): Cloud and on-premises HSMs validated to FIPS 140-3, including Thales Luna and Entrust nShield, with high availability and key lifecycle management.
