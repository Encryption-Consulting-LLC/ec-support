# CBOM Secure

CBOM Secure is the Encryption Consulting (EC) product for cryptographic discovery and inventory. It builds a Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) in CycloneDX format and shows risk and post-quantum readiness. This section covers setup, scanning, reading results, and exports.
